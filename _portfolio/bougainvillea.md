In the warm Mediterranean climate, village balconies are often festooned with cascading *Bougainvillea*. In the summer sun, the rich magenta and fandango hues seem to throb with colour.

I have focused on a single cluster of three bracts, drawing out its vivid tints alongside the waxy yellow of the flowers. These colours are then carried into the geometric forms, creating a dialogue between the organic shapes of the plant and the precision of the composition.
